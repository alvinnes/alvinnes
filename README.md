## 💫 About Me

An ambitious and disciplined 11th-grade Computer Network and Telecommunications Engineering student with a strong track record in technical competitions.

Having secured **1st Place** in the Regency-level *LKS IT-Network Systems Administration* and finishing **Top 4** at the Provincial level, I possess hands-on experience in configuring complex Cisco network infrastructures, optimizing routing protocols (OSPF/EIGRP), and securing Linux/Windows server environments. 

Beyond systems and networking, I actively explore software development, focusing on frontend layouts, JavaScript, and backend logic with PHP/Laravel. Passionate about bridging the gap between infrastructure and software engineering, I am actively preparing for industry workflows and seeking opportunities to grow as an aspiring **DevOps** or **Software Engineer**.
